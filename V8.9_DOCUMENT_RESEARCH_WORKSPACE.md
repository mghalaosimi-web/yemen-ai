# Yemen AI v8.9 — Document Research Workspace

## New capabilities
- Multi-document workspace scoped by document owner.
- Evidence-only comparison across 2–5 uploaded documents.
- Automatic page-aware document outline.
- Citation tightening: answer page citations are selected from evidence actually used in synthesis, not every retrieved page.
- Ownership checks remain mandatory for listing, outlining and comparing documents.

## Research workflow
Upload -> index by page -> select one or more documents -> ask -> retrieve evidence -> compare/summarize -> expose page evidence.

The workspace remains offline-first and does not claim facts outside selected document evidence.
